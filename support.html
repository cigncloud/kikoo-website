Before you write

Please include your device model, iOS version, Kikoo version, and a short description of what happened. Never send passwords, card numbers, Apple Account credentials, or private photos.

Purchases

Something went wrong with a purchase?

  1. Check that you are signed in to the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
  2. Open Kikoo's purchase screen and choose Recover purchases when available.
  3. If the item is still missing, email us with the Apple order number and the product name. Do not attach a receipt containing personal payment details.

Payments are processed by Apple. Apple reviews and decides refund requests. You can also use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

Quick answers

Do I need an account to take photos?

No. The core camera and creative session can be used locally. An account is only needed for optional backup and cloud economy features.

Where are my photos stored?

Session work is local by default. You choose when to save to Photos or share through the system share sheet.

Can I delete my account data?

Email us from the address associated with your account. We will confirm the request and explain what can be deleted, including any purchase records that must be retained for accounting.

How do I report a safety or privacy concern?

Email cigncloud@gmail.com with “Privacy” in the subject. Do not include a face photo unless our support team specifically asks for one.
