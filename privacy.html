Privacy policy

Keep the camera close. Keep the boundaries clear.

This policy explains what Kikoo Selfie Club processes, why it processes it, and which choices stay with you.

Effective date: October 11, 2026

1. Who we are

Kikoo Selfie Club is operated by Cigncloud (“Cigncloud,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy applies to the Kikoo Selfie Club app and the support pages at cigncloud.github.io/kikoo-website.

Questions about this policy can be sent to cigncloud@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • Camera frames, photos, face landmarks, and voice recordings are designed to stay on your device during ordinary sessions.
  • We do not use your photos to build a face database or sell them to advertisers.
  • Optional account features can store compact progress, rewards, wallet, ownership, and purchase state. They are not a cloud photo gallery.
  • Apple processes App Store payments. We do not receive your payment card number.
  • Optional privacy-safe analytics are used only where the app asks for the relevant consent.

3. Information processed by the app

Camera, microphone, and Photos

With your permission, Kikoo accesses the camera for capture and live effects. Microphone access is used only for features that need recorded audio. Photos access is used when you choose to save or import media. Camera frames, face landmarks, masks, and temporary editing resources are processed on the device for ordinary use and are not uploaded by default.

Optional account and backup

If you sign in and enable optional backup, we process an account identifier and compact records such as challenge progress, trophy dates, cloud wallet state, content ownership, and synchronization status. Individual camera photos, raw face landmarks, voice recordings, and per-attempt media timelines are outside this backup promise.

Purchases

Apple handles payment and billing details. To verify an in-app purchase and grant the item safely, Kikoo may process a signed Apple transaction, product identifier, transaction identifier, environment, account binding token, and purchase or refund status. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, attachments, and technical details you decide to provide. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or private photos unless a support agent asks for a specific diagnostic image.

Diagnostics and analytics

Where enabled by the app and permitted by your choices, we may receive privacy-safe event counts and technical diagnostics such as app version, device class, operating system version, and error state. We do not send face images, face landmarks, or voice recordings as ordinary analytics.

4. Why we process information

We use the information above to provide the camera and editing features, save results when you ask us to, maintain optional account sync, verify and recover purchases, respond to support, protect the service from abuse, and improve reliability. We do not use camera content for advertising.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the selected feature, with Apple for App Store transactions, or when required by law. We do not sell personal information. When you use the system share sheet or save to Photos, the destination is chosen by you and governed by that service's terms.

6. Storage and deletion

Local photos and edits remain under your device and Photos settings. You can delete local app data through the operating system. For optional account data, email cigncloud@gmail.com with an account deletion request. We will verify the request, delete or de-identify eligible account data, and retain only records required for security, legal, tax, fraud prevention, or Apple transaction reconciliation.

7. Security and changes

We use access controls and authenticated service calls for optional account and purchase operations. No online service can promise absolute security. We may update this policy when the app or law changes; the effective date at the top will show the latest version.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact cigncloud@gmail.com. Please include enough information to locate your request, but do not include passwords or payment card numbers.
